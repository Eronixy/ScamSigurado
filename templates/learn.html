How to Spot a Scam 🚩

  • Too good to be true offers
  • Poor grammar or spelling errors
  • Urgent pressure to act fast
  • Requests for upfront payment
  • Unverified or suspicious sources

Stay Protected 🔒

  • Never share your OTP or passwords with anyone
  • Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • Pause and think before clicking any link
  • Use ScamAlam to scan suspicious messages

Report Scams 📞

If you've been scammed, report it to help stop criminals.
